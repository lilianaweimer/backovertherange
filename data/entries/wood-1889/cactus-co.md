There are between Fountain and Pueblo, side-track stations as follows: Buttes, Wigwam, Piñon, Cactus
and Nada. These places are useful to the railroad and convenient for the residents of the
surrounding country, but they possess little or no interest for the traveler. All the way from
Denver to Pueblo the traveler has the Front Range of mountains on his right, to the west, while on
his left are the great plains. Below Colorado Springs the country is very fertile, and good crops
are grown wherever water for irrigation can be procured. Water is scarce, however, and only a small
part of the land is under cultivation.
