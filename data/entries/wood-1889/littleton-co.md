Littleton is prettily situated on the east bank of the Platte River, is the centre of a good
agricultural country, and is destined to be the location of the suburban residences of many of
Denver’s best citizens. Already an adequate suburban train service has been inaugurated for the
convenience of persons having country homes at this delightful spot. (Population, 300. Distance from
Denver, 10 miles. Elevation, 5,372 feet.)
