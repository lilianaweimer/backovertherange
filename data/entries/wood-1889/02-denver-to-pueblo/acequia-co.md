A small station for the accommodation of ranchmen. Here the High Line Canal, one of those great
irrigating ditches characteristic of Colorado, crosses the track and takes its winding way to the
northeast over the rolling plains, having under its fertilizing power at least a hundred thousand
acres of otherwise arid land. (Population, nominal. Distance from Denver, 17 miles. Elevation, 5,530
feet.)
