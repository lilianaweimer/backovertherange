With such an approach, Denver must needs be something more than ordinary not to strike the traveler
as a discord in the grand harmony of the scene. It is a fact, and it is a pleasure for the writer to
record it, that Denver is never a disappointment. What its peculiar charms may be, and how it
appears to the stranger within its gates, will be described in the succeeding chapter.
