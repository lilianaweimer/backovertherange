Back Over the Range

What the old guidebooks said, and what is there now.

Entries are transcribed by hand from public-domain travel guides, mostly nineteenth-century railroad guides to the American West. Each place shows what each guide said about it, with page numbers, and, where known, what the place is like today.

The project began with an undergraduate thesis following Stanley Wood's Over the Range to the Golden Gate and Ernest Ingersoll's The Crest of the Continent by car, from Squamish to Los Angeles to Denver.
