Back Over the Range

What the old guidebooks said, and what is there now.

Entries are transcribed by hand from public-domain travel guides, starting with railroad guides to the American West. Each place shows what each guide said about it, with page numbers, and, where known, short sourced facts about the place today.

This is a record of what old books said, not a travel guide. Locations may be approximate, and some places are on private land, closed or gone. Check before you go.
